RILO — 400 × 400 m map (concept)

Top-down layout. 4-way rotational symmetry: every squad gets the same distances, each quadrant has its own look.
Centre — Rilo City (150 m wide circle, city blocks = cover and rooftops), ringed by a canal with 8 bridges (chokepoints). Central plaza: Hack Terminal ring (Objective 1, shared) + Central Vault.
4 squad spawns in the corners, ~210 m from the centre. Capture zone per squad in the neighbouring region (Objective 2), same distance for all. Energy cores spread through Forest / Dockyard / Hydro / Canyon (Objective 3).
Extraction points (dashed white): Outposts and Ruins on the 4 sides — each sits between two squads; one is revealed when the first Vault opens.
High ground ridges between regions, ring road + spokes for rotations, sea all around.
